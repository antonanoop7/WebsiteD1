My method

The person who completes the most questions wins.

The answer is genuinely simple. But you have to get the most out of every single question. Here are the four steps I followed for every topic.

01

Understand the basics perfectly

Get the fundamentals of the topic down properly. Don't talk yourself into thinking you understand when you don't. Everything else is built on this.

02

A few textbook questions

Do a couple of questions from your maths textbook to get comfortable with the topic. Don't spend too long here. It's a warm-up, not the main event.

03
90% of your time

Exam-style questions. Not homework.

This is where you should spend most of your time on each topic. Do as many as you can while making sure you understand every one. If you don't understand something, get help. That's where tutors and teachers come in.

04

Timed exams, properly marked

Sit timed exams under exam conditions and get them marked by a teacher or tutor. Then work out exactly what you still need to do to perfect your maths.

Give it 100%. Sit on the same question for 30 minutes if you have to, trying different methods.
